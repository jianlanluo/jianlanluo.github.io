Research

My research focuses on building principled, scalable robotic learning systems that achieve reliable, high-performance behavior in complex real-world environments. I aim to move beyond isolated models toward end-to-end learning systems that can autonomously operate, adapt, and continually improve through physical interaction. A central theme of my work is a unified learning framework that integrates vision-language models, world models, and reinforcement learning into a continuous data flywheel—enabling agents to learn from multimodal experience, anticipate the consequences of actions, and iteratively refine behavior through real-world feedback.


Key Research Themes:

  • Generalist Robot Learning — Developing VLA and world models that enable compositional task understanding, predictive planning, and generalization across tasks, objects, and embodiments
  • Real-World Reinforcement Learning — Designing sample-efficient RL systems with human feedback that achieve superhuman reliability and precision in contact-rich manipulation, enabling practical deployment in industrial settings
  • Learning-Based Robotic Systems — Building open-source, end-to-end robotic learning frameworks (e.g., SERL, HIL-SERL) that tightly integrate learning algorithms with physical constraints, enabling stable, reproducible, and scalable real-world deployment
